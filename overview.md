# Trainer Hub Overview

Trainer Hub is a premium Android application for verified personal trainers who manage one-to-one clients. The app helps a trainer review today’s sessions, monitor overdue payments, build structured training plans, record session notes, and handle privacy actions such as consent, export, and deletion requests. It is intentionally trainer-first because that keeps the assessment focused on a coherent professional workflow instead of splitting effort across client and trainer experiences. The product avoids generic fitness-tracker clutter and instead concentrates on the activities that matter most in a premium coaching relationship: planning, session delivery, client oversight, in-platform payments, and compliant client data management.
